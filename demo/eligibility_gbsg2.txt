Inclusion Criteria:
- Primary operable breast cancer (T1-3, no distant metastases)
- Histologically proven positive axillary lymph nodes (at least one positive node)
- Adjuvant chemotherapy with cyclophosphamide, methotrexate and fluorouracil (CMF)
- Written informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
- Distant metastases at diagnosis

Control arm:
- No adjuvant hormonal therapy (chemotherapy alone)
